E-COMMERCE CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

==================================================
ORDER TRACKING
==================================================

Customers can track their orders using the Order Tracking page on the website.

After an order is shipped, the customer receives a tracking number through email or SMS.

Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days after shipment.

Express delivery usually takes 1 to 2 business days after shipment.

Delivery times may vary depending on the customer's location, courier service, weather conditions, and public holidays.

If the tracking information has not been updated for more than 48 hours, the customer should contact customer support.

An order marked as "Out for Delivery" is usually delivered on the same business day, but delivery can sometimes be delayed.

Customers can track their order using their order number and registered email address.

==================================================
ORDER STATUS
==================================================

An order can have several statuses.

"Order Placed" means the order has been successfully created but has not yet been processed.

"Processing" means the warehouse is preparing the order.

"Shipped" means the package has been handed over to the courier.

"Out for Delivery" means the courier is attempting to deliver the package.

"Delivered" means the courier has confirmed delivery.

"Cancelled" means the order has been cancelled and will not be delivered.

==================================================
RETURNS
==================================================

Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging.

Products should include original accessories, manuals, tags, and invoices when applicable.

Some products, such as personalized products, customized products, and certain hygiene products, cannot be returned.

Customers must create a return request through the Orders section of the website.

The return request may be rejected if the product is damaged due to customer misuse.

Products received in a damaged or defective condition may qualify for a replacement or refund.

==================================================
RETURN PROCESS
==================================================

To return a product, the customer should open the Orders section.

The customer should select the order that contains the product.

The customer should select "Return Item".

The customer must select a return reason.

The customer may be required to upload photographs for damaged or defective products.

After the return request is approved, the customer receives instructions for returning the product.

The courier may collect the product from the customer's address.

Once the returned product is received and inspected, the refund process begins.

==================================================
REFUNDS
==================================================

After a returned product is received and inspected, the refund is normally processed within 5 to 7 business days.

The refund is sent to the original payment method.

Bank processing times can cause the refund to take additional time to appear in the customer's account.

Shipping charges are generally non-refundable.

If a customer paid using a credit card, the refund will normally be returned to the same credit card.

If a customer paid using UPI, the refund will normally be processed to the original payment method.

Customers should contact support if a refund has not appeared after the expected processing period.

==================================================
ORDER CANCELLATION
==================================================

Customers can cancel an order before it is shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, it cannot normally be cancelled.

If an order has already been shipped, the customer can request a return after delivery if the product is eligible for return.

Cancellation requests can be submitted from the Orders section.

If payment was already completed, the refund will be processed after cancellation according to the refund policy.

==================================================
DAMAGED PRODUCTS
==================================================

If a customer receives a damaged product, they should report it within 48 hours of delivery.

The customer should provide photographs showing the damaged product and packaging.

The support team reviews the damage report.

Depending on availability, the customer may receive a replacement or refund.

Customers should keep the original packaging until the issue is resolved.

==================================================
WRONG PRODUCT
==================================================

If a customer receives a product different from the product ordered, they should create a return request.

The customer should select "Wrong Product Received" as the return reason.

The customer may be asked to provide photographs of the received product.

The company may arrange a pickup of the incorrect product.

A replacement may be shipped after the incorrect product is returned.

If a replacement is unavailable, the customer may receive a refund.

==================================================
MISSING ITEMS
==================================================

If an order contains multiple products and one product is missing, the customer should contact support.

The customer should provide the order number and details of the missing item.

The support team will check the shipment information.

If the item was shipped separately, the customer will receive separate tracking information.

If the item was not shipped, the company may arrange another shipment or provide a refund.

==================================================
PAYMENT METHODS
==================================================

The store accepts credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and net banking.

Some products may support additional payment methods depending on the customer's location.

Payment information is encrypted and securely processed.

The store does not store complete card numbers.

Customers should never share their card PIN, CVV, password, or one-time password with customer support.

==================================================
PAYMENT FAILURE
==================================================

If a payment fails, the customer should first check whether the amount was deducted from their bank account.

If the amount was not deducted, the customer can try the payment again.

If the amount was deducted but the order was not created, the payment will normally be automatically reversed.

Customers should contact support if a failed payment has not been reversed within the expected banking period.

Customers should provide the transaction reference number when contacting support about a payment issue.

==================================================
CASH ON DELIVERY
==================================================

Cash on Delivery is available only for eligible products and locations.

The availability of Cash on Delivery is displayed during checkout.

Customers should pay the exact amount requested by the delivery agent whenever possible.

Cash on Delivery orders may have an order value limit.

Some products may not support Cash on Delivery.

==================================================
PRODUCT INFORMATION
==================================================

Product information is available on each product page.

Product pages may include price, specifications, dimensions, weight, materials, warranty information, and available colors.

Customers should check product specifications before placing an order.

Product images may appear slightly different from the actual product because of lighting and screen settings.

==================================================
PRODUCT AVAILABILITY
==================================================

A product marked "In Stock" can normally be ordered.

A product marked "Out of Stock" cannot currently be ordered.

Customers may choose the "Notify Me" option when available.

The customer will receive a notification when the product becomes available again.

Product availability can change without notice because inventory changes continuously.

==================================================
PRODUCT PRICE
==================================================

Product prices are displayed on the product page.

Prices may change because of promotions, discounts, seasonal offers, or inventory changes.

The price displayed during checkout is the final price before payment.

Customers should verify the final amount before completing an order.

==================================================
DISCOUNTS AND COUPONS
==================================================

Discount coupons can be applied during checkout.

A coupon may have an expiration date.

Some coupons are valid only for specific products or categories.

Some coupons require a minimum order value.

Only one promotional coupon may be used per order unless the promotion explicitly allows multiple coupons.

Expired or invalid coupons cannot be applied.

==================================================
DELIVERY
==================================================

The company delivers to most major cities and towns.

Delivery availability depends on the customer's postal code.

Some remote locations may have longer delivery times.

Customers can enter their postal code on the product page to check delivery availability.

Public holidays and severe weather can affect delivery times.

==================================================
DELIVERY ADDRESS
==================================================

Customers can change their delivery address before the order is shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, the delivery address normally cannot be changed.

Customers should verify their address before completing the order.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses may cause delivery delays.

==================================================
MISSED DELIVERY
==================================================

If the customer is unavailable during delivery, the courier may attempt delivery again.

The number of delivery attempts depends on the courier service.

Customers may receive a notification about another delivery attempt.

If all delivery attempts fail, the package may be returned to the warehouse.

==================================================
CUSTOMER ACCOUNT
==================================================

Customers can create an account using their email address or mobile number.

Customers can update their profile information from Account Settings.

Customers should use a strong password for their account.

Customers should never share their account password or one-time password with anyone.

==================================================
PASSWORD RESET
==================================================

Customers who forget their password can select "Forgot Password" on the login page.

A password reset link or verification code will be sent to the registered email address or mobile number.

The customer must follow the verification steps before creating a new password.

==================================================
WARRANTY
==================================================

Warranty coverage depends on the product and manufacturer.

Warranty duration is displayed on the product page when applicable.

Warranty normally covers manufacturing defects.

Physical damage caused by misuse may not be covered by warranty.

Customers should contact support with the order number and product details when requesting warranty service.

==================================================
PRIVACY
==================================================

Customer personal information must be protected.

Customer support agents should only access information required to resolve the customer's request.

Customers should never be asked to provide passwords, card PINs, CVV numbers, or one-time passwords.

One customer's private order information must never be disclosed to another customer.

==================================================
CUSTOMER SUPPORT
==================================================

Customer support is available Monday to Friday from 9 AM to 6 PM.

Customers can contact support through the Help Center.

Customers should provide their order number when contacting support about an existing order.

Support requests are handled according to their priority and type.

Urgent payment and security issues may receive higher priority.

==================================================
SECURITY
==================================================

Customers should not share passwords, card PINs, CVV numbers, or OTPs.

The company will never ask customers to share their account password.

Customers should be cautious of suspicious emails requesting payment information.

Customers should report suspected fraudulent activity to customer support.

==================================================
COMMON EDGE CASES
==================================================

If an order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date, the customer should check the latest tracking information.

If tracking information has not changed for more than 48 hours, the customer should contact support.

If a customer receives a damaged product, they should report it within 48 hours.

If a customer wants to cancel an order after shipment, cancellation is normally unavailable and a return may be requested after delivery.

If a customer wants to return a product after 30 days, the product may not qualify under the standard return policy.

If a customer receives the wrong product, they should create a return request using the "Wrong Product Received" reason.

If a refund is delayed beyond the expected processing period, the customer should contact support.

If a payment was deducted but an order was not created, the payment may be automatically reversed.
